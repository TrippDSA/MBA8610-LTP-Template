# Four-decisions journal

Write an entry whenever the team makes one of the four calls: **what to build, what "good enough" means, who is accountable, and when to stop.** Date it, name who decided, and point to the evidence: a pull request, a commit, or a transcript in `ai-logs/`. The four-decisions write-ups in D2, D3, and D4 draw from this file, so an entry written the day of the decision is worth more than a paragraph reconstructed the week the deliverable is due.

Copy the block below for each entry. Newest entries go at the top.
